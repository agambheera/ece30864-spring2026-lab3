QUESTION A

Pros of Agile Development
- It allows for a very iterative approach by breaking the project down to several stages.
- It allows developers to constantly collaborate with customers, constantly adding value to customers.

Cons of Agile Development
- The development cycle is a very high time commitment because it involves ongoing involvement from clients and teams.
- The development cycle can also be challenging to implement for projects that need strict upfront planning.

QUESTION B

Roles and Responsibilites of Scrum Master
- The Scrum Master is responsible for all scrum-related tasks like Jira tooling and facilitating sprints and meetings.

Roles and Responsibilites of Product Owner
- The Product Owner is responible for setting the direction for the team and handles releases and stakeholder management.

QUESTION C

Types of Meetings During a Sprint Cycle

Sprint Planning Meeting:
- During this meeting, what to complete in the coming sprint is dicussed.
- During this meeting, which stories to move from the backlog to the current sprint is discussed.

Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting:
- During this meeting, team members discuss work completed.
- During this meeting, team members discuss their work plan for the day and any blockers.

Sprint Retrospective Meeting:
- During this meeting, team members dicuss work that was completed.
- During this meeting, team members also discuss incomplete work and move it to the backlog.

QUESTION D

Epic vs Story vs Bug vs Task

Epic
- An epic is a large body of work that can be broken down into several smaller stories.

Story
- A story is a software system requirement that is expressed in a few short sentences.

Bug 
- A bug is a specific type of issue used to track defects or problems in software that prevent it from working as intended. 

Task
- A task is a standard work item that represents a specific piece of work that needs to be done.
